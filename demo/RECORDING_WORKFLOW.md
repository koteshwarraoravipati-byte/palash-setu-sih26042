# PALASH Setu demo recording workflow

## Target length

Three minutes. Record the phone/tablet screen and a small presenter voiceover. Use one continuous take for the offline proof and short retakes for narration if needed.

## Before recording

- Install the APK on Android 9+.
- Load only native-reviewed Santhali rows and approved audio.
- Confirm the device has the Hindi offline speech pack.
- Turn on airplane mode and show the airplane icon.
- Close unrelated notifications and apps.
- Keep a Santhali-speaking reviewer present for the final output check.
- Prepare the exact phrase from the approved test row.

## Shot list and narration

| Time | Screen action | Narration / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Show problem title and PALASH Setu home screen | “Hindi-medium teachers in tribal-area schools need a safe bridge to deliver foundational learning in the child’s mother tongue.” |
| 0:20–0:35 | Pull down quick settings and show airplane mode | “This demonstration is offline. No cloud API is being used during the classroom flow.” |
| 0:35–1:05 | Open Live Class and tap the approved phrase or speak Hindi | “The teacher can speak a Hindi classroom prompt. The device recognises it locally, then performs a bounded classroom phrase lookup.” |
| 1:05–1:25 | Show Hindi, Santhali, transliteration, and play audio | “The output comes from a versioned, native-reviewed Santhali content pack. The reviewer confirms the wording and pronunciation.” |
| 1:25–1:45 | Speak an unknown phrase or choose a deliberately unmatched input | “When there is no approved match, PALASH Setu refuses to invent a translation and asks the teacher to choose a safe phrase.” |
| 1:45–2:10 | Open Lessons and show NIPUN/FLN outcome | “Each lesson is tied to a foundational literacy or numeracy outcome.” |
| 2:10–2:35 | Open Teaching Aids, generate worksheet, move through flashcards | “The teacher can generate a bilingual worksheet preview and use visual flashcards without internet.” |
| 2:35–2:50 | Show pack version and review metadata | “Content is versioned, reviewable, and expandable to Ho and Mundari only after separate native validation.” |
| 2:50–3:00 | Show Team Sprit title card | “PALASH Setu: a practical offline bridge from Hindi-trained teachers to mother-tongue learning.” |

## Evidence overlays

Show only measured values: device model, Android version, offline status, approved phrase count, audio count, APK size, and recognition-to-lookup latency. Never display a target latency as if it were a measured result.

## Judging-safe claims

Say “prototype,” “bounded phrase lookup,” “offline preference,” and “native review required” until the complete device pilot is finished. Do not say “full AI translation,” “100% accurate,” or “sub-three-second” without test evidence.
