# PALASH Setu Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 August 2026**

PALASH Setu is an offline educational prototype created by Team Sprit for the SIH26042 problem statement. It helps primary-school teachers use Hindi classroom prompts with a Santhali-first teaching content pack.

## Information handled by the app

- **Microphone audio:** When the teacher chooses Hindi voice input, Android speech recognition receives microphone input for that recognition session. PALASH Setu does not intentionally store, upload, sell, or share the recording from inside the app.
- **On-device speech result:** The recognized Hindi text is used locally to match an approved classroom phrase. The current prototype does not send lesson text, voice recordings, or student information to a server.
- **Lesson progress:** The current prototype does not require an account and does not collect a student profile, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or payment information.

Android speech-recognition availability and processing can vary by device and installed language pack. Users should review the device speech-service settings and permissions.

## Permissions

The app requests microphone permission only for the optional Hindi voice-input feature. The main lesson, worksheet, and teaching-aid screens do not require microphone access.

## Children and schools

PALASH Setu is an educational prototype. Do not enter names, photographs, health information, contact details, or other sensitive information into the prototype. Teachers and school administrators should supervise classroom use.

## Third-party services

The current prototype contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system, or in-app purchase system. Android system speech recognition may be provided by a device-level service subject to that service's own terms and privacy policy.

This policy will be updated before a production release or if the app begins using synchronization, analytics, accounts, or cloud speech services. For project questions, contact Team Sprit through the public GitHub repository:
